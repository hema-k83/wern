Privacy Policy — Wern

Last updated:

Wern ("we", "our", or "us") operates this website (the "Site") and the Wern mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user information when you use our App or visit our Site.

Information We Collect

On the App

  • Analytics: We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as app interactions, device information, and performance metrics. This helps us improve the learning experience.
  • Advertising: We use Google AdMob in the App to display non-personalized ads. AdMob may collect certain device information (such as device identifiers) to serve ads. All ads comply with the Google Play Families Policy.
  • No personal data: We do not collect personal information such as names, addresses, or contact details via the App.

On the Website

  • We do not serve ads on the Site.
  • We do not collect personal information directly from visitors through the Site.
  • Standard server logs (such as browser type, pages visited, and time of visit) may be collected automatically for security and performance purposes.

Children’s Privacy

The App is designed for children and complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

  • Ads in the App are non-personalized and appropriate for children.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
  • The Website is intended for parents and guardians to learn about the App. We do not knowingly collect information from children on the Site.

How We Use Information

  • To understand how the App is used and improve learning features.
  • To monitor performance and fix technical issues.
  • To display non-personalized ads in the App (not the Site).
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the Site.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell or share children’s personal data.
  • Analytics data is processed by Google Firebase according to their Privacy Policy.
  • Ads in the App are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and process information in accordance with their Privacy Policy.
